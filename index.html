ESG Performance Dashboard

This dashboard helps B.C. businesses understand and compare their Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) performance against provincial and national benchmarks. Use the tabs above to explore Environmental and Social indicators by industry.

🌱

Environment

Track energy consumption and greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions across B.C. industries. Compare against national benchmarks per employee.

👥

Social

Analyse workforce demographics, wage equity, gender diversity, and board representation across Canadian industries.

📋

Governance

Governance indicators are under development. Coming soon: board composition, audit practices, and transparency metrics.

Methodology Notes

  • Energy and GHG values are normalised per employee to allow cross-industry comparison.
  • Gender categories (Men+, Women+) follow Statistics Canada's standard designations.
  • All dollar values are in nominal Canadian dollars unless otherwise stated.
  • Industry classifications follow the North American Industry Classification System (NAICS).
