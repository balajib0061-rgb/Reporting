Process Description — Blue Ammonia Pathway

Stage Description Key Input Key Output Environmental Note
1. Feedstock Natural Gas Extraction & Processing CH₄ (fossil) Purified NG Upstream methane leakage (fugitive emissions)
2. Reforming Steam Methane Reforming (SMR) at ~900°C CH₄ + H₂O Syngas (H₂ + CO + CO₂) Major CO₂ generation point (~45% of total)
3. Shift Water-Gas Shift Reaction (HTS + LTS) CO + H₂O CO₂ + H₂ (enriched) Increases H₂ yield; additional CO₂ produced
4. CO₂ Capture Amine Scrubbing (MDEA / MEA) Syngas with CO₂ Pure H₂ + CO₂ stream Captures 85–92% of process CO₂; energy-intensive
5. CCS CO₂ Compression, Transport & Geological Storage Captured CO₂ CO₂ stored (geological) Pipeline infrastructure; long-term monitoring
6. ASU Cryogenic Air Separation Unit Atmospheric air Pure N₂ (99.99%) High electricity demand (~0.2 GJ/tN₂)
7. Synthesis Haber–Bosch Loop (150–300 bar, Fe catalyst) H₂ + N₂ NH₃ (anhydrous) High pressure/temperature; recycle loop losses
